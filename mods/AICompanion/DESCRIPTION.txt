A viking companion who follows you and fights beside you, with an AI deciding how it fights.

- Press J to summon your companion (or open its menu); E on it opens the menu too.
- The mod does the fighting itself: moving, swinging, blocking and shooting at full speed with the game's own animations.
- In a fight it asks Jev (TypeSafe's decision model) about once a second: attack whom, defend you, back off, fall back to you, flee, bow or melee, drink a potion. Paste your Jev API key (console.typesafe.ai) in its menu. It costs about 12 cents an hour of fighting.
- No key, no internet, or Jev unsure: a built-in brain decides, so it never stands idle.
- Its menu: health and what it is doing, orders (follow, stay, guard a spot), fighting style (aggressive, balanced, defensive, passive), when to fall back, potions, its last decisions and how sure Jev was.
- It has stamina like you: swings, blocks and running cost it, and with none it cannot swing or run and its guard breaks.
- Your boss power (Forsaken power) reaches it too when it is within 10 m of you.
- Lives like a player: it needs food for health and stamina, its skills rise as it fights, the weather wets and chills it, and it repairs its gear at a workbench.
- Rides along on your boat, in a seat.
- Choose how it looks (body, hair, beard, skin, hair colour, or copy yours); its face shows in the party panel.
- Like a player: food, skills, rested, carry weight, eitr and staffs; it recovers its own tombstone, picks up loot, hunts, cooks and crafts its own gear.
- Talk to it in chat: "Rádvar, follow me", "Rádvar go home". Up to 3 companions each.
- Lives its own life at home: gathers, stores what it finds, repairs and upgrades its gear, and catches up on all that while you are away. Tell it to follow you for an adventure.
- A home like a player's: give it a bed (it wakes there after falling) and chests (it keeps what it gathers there and takes better gear from them).
- Gather: it chops wood, mines stone and ore, and picks wild berries and mushrooms around its bed, with the tools you give it.
- It follows you through portals and opens doors.
- Give it weapons, armour, arrows and healing potions (Open its inventory): it wears the best and its armour protects it.
- If it falls, its gear goes into a tombstone, marked with a skull on everyone's map, and it wakes in its bed 30 s later.
- It shows on the minimap and the big map (a teal player icon with its name), for everyone with the mod.
- With PartyHud, your companion shows in the party panel under you: health, distance, direction and what it is doing.
- Multiplayer: it belongs to whoever summoned it, and its brain runs on their game. Everyone in the world needs the mod (the host too), or the game deletes saved companions.
