New: companions have stamina. Swings and blocks cost what they cost you, running drains it, and it comes back after a short rest; when it runs out it cannot swing or run and its guard breaks, so it backs off to get its breath (Jev is told how much it has). And your boss power reaches companions within 10 m of you, as it does other players (Eikthyr's lighter running, Bonemass's toughness, Yagluth's strength...). The menu has tabs. Overview (what it is doing, against whom, how sure Jev is, its kills, fights and potions, Come here), Orders, Inventory (what it wears and its bag with icons: click to take, and the useful things in yours: click to give), Brain (the Jev key, cost, how often to ask, how sure Jev must be) and Debug (every decision with Jev's odds for each answer and the exact request and answer, copy buttons, and an optional log file). Companions show on the minimap and the big map (a teal player icon with their name), yours and other players', for everyone with the mod. When a companion falls, a skull marks the spot on the map of everyone with the mod (players who join later are told too); it goes away once its gear crate has been emptied. With PartyHud 1.7.0, each player's companion shows in the party panel under them: health, distance, direction and what it is doing.

First version: summon a companion (J), give it gear, and it follows you and fights beside you. Jev (with your API key) decides its tactics in a fight; a built-in brain does without one.
