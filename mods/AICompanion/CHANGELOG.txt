Every text wraps to fit, nothing is cut off, and the checkboxes are real checkboxes. The settings are reordered and described in plain words (advanced Jev settings marked as such). Its portrait is taken while it stands still, facing you. Fix: E on a companion's tombstone moved everything into your inventory (the game took it for yours), and the companion woke with nothing: now E gives its things back to it if it is alive nearby (it wears them again), and otherwise opens the tombstone like a chest. New, a companion more like a player: it goes back to its tombstone after it falls and takes all its things back (when it is near home, or near you while following), and wears them again. It picks up what is on its pick-up list (Inventory tab: ore and metal, wood, stone, food, creature drops; gear and everything else off by default) as it passes by, like a player's auto-pickup, and goes out of its way for fresh drops near it when it is not fighting: the ore and stone you mine, the wood you chop, berries, and the loot after a fight, also on adventures (never what a player drops from their inventory; Orders tab). At home it hunts deer and boar for meat and hides, cooks its raw food on a cooking station and takes it off before it burns, and makes new gear at its workbench when it has the materials (better armour, shield, weapon, a higher-tier axe or pickaxe, a bow, arrows), besides upgrading. It gets Rested by a while under a roof by a fire (heals and gets its breath back faster), has a player's carry weight (over it, it cannot run), uses eitr from food to fight with staffs, and defends its home against raids. Talk to it in chat: start a message with its name ("Rádvar, follow me", "Rádvar go home"); with a Jev key Jev understands what you mean. Up to 3 companions each (Companion, MaxCompanions), switched between in the menu. Friends can give it orders if you allow it. WhileAway: Real, where fights while you are away can go badly and it can fall.

Stronger nudging: walk towards a companion or bump it and it gets out of the way at once: a shove sends it aside the moment you touch it, it runs about 3 m clear, and meanwhile you pass through it rather than being stopped.

New: companions live their own lives. "Live at home" (the old Gather): with no jobs ticked it decides for itself (wood with an axe, stone and ore with a pickaxe, foraging when low on food), stores what it finds in its chests, repairs, and upgrades its gear at its workbench or forge when it has the materials (the game's costs and station levels, from its bag and its chests). Tell it to follow you for an adventure. While nobody is near, it goes on living: when you come back it catches up on the work it would have done (trees felled, rocks mined, berries picked, at a player's pace with its tools, into its chests) and, with Companion, WhileAway: Mild, fights off a few creatures of its biome and keeps their drops; it tells you what it did. Fixes: when a companion fell, only 4 of its item stacks fitted in its tombstone and the rest was lost: now the tombstone takes everything it carried, as a player's does (the game's own move, which makes the tombstone as big as the bag); upgrades, repairs and wear on its gear are now saved (they were lost when its area reloaded); stakes hurt a companion that walks into them and no longer wear themselves down on it; a companion steps aside when you walk into it; its picture is taken once (and again when its looks or gear change), not as a live feed; its food shows in the party panel (PartyHud 1.7.2).

New: a companion lives like a player. Food: it has a player's 25 health and 75 stamina, and up to three foods add theirs and heal it (it eats from its bag and fetches food from its chests; without food it does not heal). Skills: each weapon's skill rises as its hits land and makes it hit harder, with the player's formulas; it loses 5% when it falls. Weather: wet in the rain, sheltered, warm by a fire, cold, freezing without frost resistance, as you. Its armour's resistances protect it. Repairs: it takes worn gear to a station that can repair it. Riding along: when you board a boat it takes a seat and rides with you, and steps off when you do. Looks: a Looks tab to choose its body, hair, beard, skin and hair colour (or copy yours), and its face shows in its menu and in the party panel (PartyHud 1.7.1). Give it a bed and chests of its own (Home tab, Assign, then E on a bed or a chest). When it falls, its gear goes into a tombstone like a player's (you can take it all back with one E) and it wakes in its bed (or beside you without one) 30 s later, with its name, looks and settings: who it is is kept with your character, per world. New order: Gather. With the jobs you tick (Work tab) it chops logs, stumps and trees, mines rocks and ore, picks wild berries, mushrooms and flowers (never your crops), or picks up everything on the ground, around its bed, with the tools in its bag (the game's tool tiers decide what it can work), and picks up what its work drops. When its bag fills it takes everything but its gear to its chests, and there it takes better armour and weapons, arrows, healing potions and missing tools. It also follows you through portals (not carrying ore), opens doors in its way, and players cannot claim its bed.

Fights, from a test fight's log: "defend you" no longer runs off after the player while enemies hit its back (it fights whoever is on it first); its fall-back health is now a rule, not advice (it falls back below it and flees below half of it, whatever Jev says); the built-in brain keeps its target and finishes the weakest enemy hitting it instead of picking the nearest (it hit one that was attacking no one); Jev's pick is trusted by its probability ("attack 41%" when the rest are lower), not its much lower confidence figure, so its clear choices are no longer overruled; Jev is told to fight what is on the companion and to defend the player only when nothing is; after each fight a summary (time, damage taken and dealt, kills, decisions) goes to the log, the menu and the Debug tab; and the skull where a companion fell no longer disappears at once.
Fix: a companion following you no longer gets stuck behind a wall of sharpened stakes (or any obstacle it cannot path over): after a few seconds without getting closer, or at once when there is no way through, it hops to a free spot beside you. And player-built stakes and spikes no longer hurt companions, as they do not hurt players.

New: companions have stamina. Swings and blocks cost what they cost you, running drains it, and it comes back after a short rest; when it runs out it cannot swing or run and its guard breaks, so it backs off to get its breath (Jev is told how much it has). And your boss power reaches companions within 10 m of you, as it does other players (Eikthyr's lighter running, Bonemass's toughness, Yagluth's strength...). The menu has tabs. Overview (what it is doing, against whom, how sure Jev is, its kills, fights and potions, Come here), Orders, Inventory (what it wears and its bag with icons: click to take, and the useful things in yours: click to give), Brain (the Jev key, cost, how often to ask, how sure Jev must be) and Debug (every decision with Jev's odds for each answer and the exact request and answer, copy buttons, and an optional log file). Companions show on the minimap and the big map (a teal player icon with their name), yours and other players', for everyone with the mod. When a companion falls, a skull marks the spot on the map of everyone with the mod (players who join later are told too); it goes away once its gear crate has been emptied. With PartyHud 1.7.0, each player's companion shows in the party panel under them: health, distance, direction and what it is doing.
