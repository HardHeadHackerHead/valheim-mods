Smelters, kilns, cooking racks, fires and fermenters can use items from nearby chests.

How it works
- Press E at a station: it adds from a chest if you have none, or opens a menu if there is a choice. Look at a station and press F to always get the menu of everything you can add.
- The menu lists items from the lesser to the greater (wood, fine wood, core wood...) and has a Fill option that tops the station up.
- Nothing is created from nothing: every item comes out of your inventory or a chest.

Settings: range (default 15 m), automatic adding, always open the menu, the menu key, and the Fill limit.
