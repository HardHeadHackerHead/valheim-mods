Plan pieces as shared ghost build orders for everyone in your party.

How it works
- Hold Left Alt and place with the hammer to plan a piece instead of building it. It shows as a see-through ghost that other players see too.
- Your own placement snaps onto a ghost, and the ghost disappears once the real piece is built.
- G selects the piece you are looking at to build more like it, and Delete removes a ghost. F9 shows or hides all ghosts.
- A panel totals the materials your orders still need.

Settings: ghost opacity, view distance, snap distance and the keys.
