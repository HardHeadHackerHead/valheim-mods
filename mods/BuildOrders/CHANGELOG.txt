New: blueprint pictures (made in the game for every blueprint) and sharing. Share on a blueprint in the Plans window (F11) sends it with its picture to everyone playing with you; it appears under "Shared with you" to place, save or dismiss. Copy code puts it on the clipboard as text for a chat, and Paste code adds one to your library. Every plan is now placed on levelled ground (no L key, which clashed with QualityOfLife's item lock), so support posts are gone, and blueprints no longer place themselves at your bed. Also: Open folder button, and the emergency hut uses less wood and keeps its fire and smoke outside.
