New: blueprints and the Plans window (F11). Pick a blueprint from your library and place it with a live green preview: look where it goes, turn it with the mouse wheel (R for 90°), raise or lower it with PgUp/PgDn, click to place. On uneven ground (or raised) the plan gets support posts down to the ground under the pieces meant to stand on it, shown in yellow in the preview, or press L to level the ground under it as soon as it is placed (removing the plan puts the ground back). Placed plans are listed with pieces left, distance, direction and the materials you have against what they need; build the nearby ones, move a plan (pick it up and put it down again) or remove it, or remove every ghost within a radius you choose. Settings for the build-all radius (now 24 m, up to 64), reach, ghost distance, opacity and how many ghosts show at once (now 600, so big builds appear whole). The mod also writes a design guide and tools for AI assistants next to the blueprints, a full list of every piece (size, snap points, cost, station, rules), a screenshot key (F12), a survey key (Ctrl+F11), and optional request files for pictures, surveys and placements.
