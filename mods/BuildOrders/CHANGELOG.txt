New: the bridge builder. Pick Bridge in the hammer's build menu (Misc), click where the bridge starts, walk or swim across while a ghost of it stretches after you, and click where it ends. You get a medieval wooden bridge (plank deck, rails, posts down to the riverbed) as ghosts to build with E; it costs nothing until you build it, and the ground is not levelled. It warns if it is very steep, the water is too deep for wooden posts, or there is no workbench in reach.
