The AI assistant's request mailbox, screenshot key and survey moved to the new Claude Tools mod (BepInEx/claude; survey is now Ctrl+F12). With Claude Tools installed, BuildOrders adds its blueprint commands to it. New: removing a partly built plan can take down what was built of it and give you the materials back. Remove in the Plans window (F11) asks: Remove ghosts only (what is built stays), or Also take down N built (the pieces come down the way the hammer takes them down, wards respected and chests with things in them left alone, and their materials go into your inventory). With everything taken down, the levelled ground goes back as it was.
