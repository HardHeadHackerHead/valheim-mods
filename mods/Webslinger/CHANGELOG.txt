0.3.0: Two suits for the trinket slot that change how you look, not what you wear (your armour stays on and keeps protecting you): the Spider-Viking suit (red and blue web suit with a mask and white eyes, leather belt with a gold buckle, bracers, leg wraps and fur-topped boots; your own helmet turns red and your cape blue with a red spider on its back) and the Venom suit (symbiote black with a huge white spider, jagged eyes and fangs, black leather and silver; your helmet and cape turn black, and your webs turn black). The Web Shooters now show on both wrists with the cartridge on the inside.
0.2.0: The Web Shooters: a utility item made at the workbench (Leather scraps, Resin, Flint); swings and zips need them (a server setting can turn that off), and a shooter shows on each wrist; the leap, the wall crawl and spider-sense work without. A plain jump is the game's own again, and never shoots a web (a web could fire right after a jump off the ground); a jump while sprinting is a Spider-Man leap far ahead, in a high, long arc. Press Jump in the air to swing.
0.1.0: First release: web-swinging from trees, cliffs and buildings (hold Jump in the air), zips to the spot under the crosshair (Sprint in the air), wall crawling, a higher Spider-Man jump, spider-sense, and a Webslinging skill. Web marks show where a web would stick. Its own web sounds, poses and flips, web patches and hanging webs, speed lines and camera motion. No stamina costs by default. Works with Quad's Parkour.
